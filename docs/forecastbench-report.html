Read this first: part of the incoherence is an elicitation artefact.

ForecastBench asked for each of the four joint outcomes in a separate prompt, so a model that forecasts every cell independently will not make them sum to one (the mean sum here is 1.61). The renormalised baseline rescales the four cells to sum to one; the marginal-versus-joint inconsistency that remains is the more robust finding, although the marginals were also elicited in their own prompts.
